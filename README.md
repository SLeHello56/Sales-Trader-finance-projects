# Quantitative Trading & Market Analysis Projects  
**Sales Trader / Front-Office oriented (Commodities & Derivatives)**

## Overview

This repository presents a set of **hands-on quantitative and trading-oriented projects**
developed with a **Front-Office mindset**, targeting roles in:

- Sales & Trading  
- Commodities Trading  
- Derivatives / Structuring  
- Market Risk & Trading Analytics  

Each project emphasizes **pricing intuition, market dynamics, volatility behavior,
and risk management**, rather than purely academic modeling.

---

## Projects

### 1️ Exotic Options Lab (Python)

**Quantitative Derivatives Pricing & Risk Management**

- Priced **Asian and Barrier options** using Monte Carlo simulations  
- Computed **Greeks** and implemented **delta-hedging strategies**  
- Analyzed **P&L attribution** and hedging performance across market scenarios  
- Focus on **path-dependency, convexity, and volatility exposure**

**Tools:** Python, NumPy, Monte Carlo methods  
**Front-Office relevance:** Exotic pricing, hedging intuition, risk monitoring

---

### 2️ Derivatives & Commodities Dashboard (Excel / VBA)

**Trading Analytics & Market Monitoring**

- Built an **automated spot–forward dashboard** for commodities  
- Integrated market data via **energy & financial APIs (EIA / Nasdaq-style)**  
- Analyzed **term structures (contango / backwardation)** and carry dynamics  
- Developed VBA tools for:
  - pricing support  
  - scenario analysis  
  - trading and hedging decisions  

**Tools:** Excel, VBA, APIs  
**Front-Office relevance:** Market monitoring, trading support tools, desk-style analytics

---

### 3️ Volatility Smile & Macro Correlation (R)

**Quantitative Market Research & Strategy Analysis**

- Modeled **equity volatility proxies (1M / 1Y)**  
- Studied interactions between volatility and macro variables:
  - inflation  
  - GDP growth  
  - unemployment  
  - central bank policy rates  
- Identified **macro–volatility regimes** using clustering and rolling correlations  
- Backtested a **low-vol vs high-vol cross-sectional strategy**, including:
  - long-only portfolios  
  - long-short implementation  
  - regime-conditional performance  

**Tools:** R, Quarto, tidyverse  
**Front-Office relevance:** Volatility regimes, macro impact on returns, relative-value logic
